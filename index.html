Help
Canvas & Eraser

Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, Alt + drag or twist to rotate, right-click to reset. In Eraser, hold Space + drag to pan and Cmd/Ctrl + Z to undo.

Text

Click text to edit inline. Drag corners to resize, top handle to rotate. Use [A] for background fill and [B] for font weight.

Background removal

Local BRIA RMBG 2.0 (≈1 GB). Downloaded once, then runs 100% offline. Images never leave your device.

Export

Save sticker exports 512×512 PNG (≤512 KB) with text. Export original saves full size without text. Output folder is in Settings.

Formats

PNG, JPG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, TIFF (+ HEIC/HEIF on macOS). Output is always PNG.
